## ATT&CK notices

The project software's MIT license does not replace dataset licenses. Copies and derived redistributions must retain MITRE's copyright designation **and the complete ATT&CK license**. The release-specific files are in `licenses/ATTACK-v16.1.txt` and `licenses/ATTACK-v17.1.txt`; the verification-time master license is also retained. Include them with exported ATT&CK-derived datasets.

The master license reviewed here states: “© 2026 The MITRE Corporation. This work is reproduced and distributed with the permission of The MITRE Corporation.” Release files may use their release-year notice; retain them verbatim. ATT&CK does not enumerate every possible behavior and does not guarantee complete defensive coverage. MITRE ATT&CK® is a trademark of The MITRE Corporation.

Raw caches, full processed graphs and embeddings are ignored by Git. Only original small synthetic fixtures, independently specified synthetic answers, attribution notices and aggregate result artifacts are committed. Collection cache sidecars record publisher URL, retrieved_at, byte size and SHA-256; TLS and Docker layer checksum verification are preserved.
